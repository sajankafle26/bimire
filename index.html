THE STORY BEHIND OUR NAMEBimire — a village, a memory, a sense of belonging.
Our roots. Your journey.

A name with a story. A journey with a purpose.

Bimire is the mountain village in Ramechhap where our founder's late father was born. That family connection inspired the name of Bimire Tour and the spirit behind our work.

We believe travel becomes meaningful when we go beyond places and connect with people — sharing everyday life, listening to local stories and discovering the beauty of Nepal together.

“Come as a guest, leave as a friend.”

Whether you dream of heritage walks, countryside journeys, trekking, wildlife or cultural exchange, we welcome you to explore Nepal with an open heart.
